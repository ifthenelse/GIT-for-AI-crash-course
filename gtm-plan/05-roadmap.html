Schedule and capacity

D1–D90 are indicative windows. D1 starts after operational approval, owner assignment, and declared initial availability. The confirmed 1–2 hours/week covers post-launch maintenance only: it does not size development or translation.

If work exceeds the horizon, move the release. Zero service cost, three complete tiers, and IT/EN parity remain mandatory. This document promises no launch date.

One person may hold several roles after capacity is checked. I2/I3 are a private review, not a reduced public launch. I4 preserves the entire scope. Initial I1 evidence does not replace the complete real-world I4 test.

Engine activation

  • E1, searchable scenarios: material in I4 and public links in I5. Impressions and visits when available; no guaranteed indexing date.
  • E2, example → course: private I2/I3 test, public I5 start. Observe whether a person understands the task and reaches the relevant exercise.
  • E3, feedback → revision → return: first I3 cycle, continuing in I5/I6. Document difficulty, reasoned decision, and verification of the correction.

No invented volumes, conversion rates, or learning improvements. Absent observations mean missing evidence, not automatic success or failure.

Twelve-month outline

  • Months 1–3: address the six initiatives in dependency order, with a conditional schedule. Continue beyond the quarter if requirements remain unmet.
  • Months 4–6: after launch, consolidate correctness, IT/EN, and sustainability; consider C10 only for an observed barrier and with free prerequisites. If launch is blocked, complete the initial scope first.
  • Months 7–9: consider C11 only with spare capacity and authorization. Do not add a fourth channel if maintenance cannot sustain it.
  • Months 10–12: reassess C16 using collected problems and tests; update the roadmap before new formats. C12/C15 remain parked without demonstrated need.
